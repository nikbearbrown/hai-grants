# Humanitarians AI: grants and collaborations

Humanitarians AI Inc. is a nonprofit that builds and evaluates AI for public benefit. This is our public list of projects we are considering and where collaborators can help. A listing is not a funding award, a submitted application, a confirmed partner, or a job offer. Dates below are sponsor deadlines; our team decisions may happen earlier.

**Want to help?** Reply privately to the person who invited you with the opportunity or BPHC idea ID, what you would like to contribute, and your availability. Current Humanitarians AI fellows can express interest in being considered for a nomination where the sponsor allows it. For public questions about the project ideas, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/hai-grants/issues/new). Do not post résumés, contact details, eligibility documents, participant records, or private data in an issue.
