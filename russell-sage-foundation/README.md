# Russell Sage Foundation

## Margaret Olivia Sage Grants: societal impacts of AI

**Status:** Seeking potential research partners; no letter submitted. The letter of inquiry is due **December 1, 2026 at 2 p.m. ET**. Invited proposals are due February 22, 2027, at 2 p.m. ET. The competition offers up to $750,000, plus 15% indirect costs, over three years.

**Research idea:** Study whether access to AI-assisted job-search tools changes outcomes for early-career workers in the United States. Separate job listings and stated employer demand from actual search behavior, interviews, and employment. A prospective study could compare an AI-assisted workflow with standard search resources, measuring time, application quality, interviews, and whether effects vary by access or experience. Job postings alone cannot establish the effect. Community partners would help shape the research questions, recruitment and public dissemination.

**Who could collaborate:** Social scientists, labor economists, workforce organizations, and research-methods partners. The foundation calls for a multidisciplinary or interdisciplinary team and says all named PIs and co-PIs must have doctorates; it also welcomes community partners, including as co-PI where eligibility is met. Do not assume an exception for a community partner without sponsor confirmation. No investigator or partner is confirmed.
