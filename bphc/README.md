# Boston Public Health Commission I³ Accelerator

Humanitarians AI is exploring separate proposals for these public wishlist ideas. No award, team placement, or data access is implied. The [City call](https://www.boston.gov/departments/boston-public-health-commission/boston-sphere) lists November 30, 2026 at 5 p.m. ET. See [ways to get involved](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Partner and Fellow arrangements are discussed privately.

## I3-22: Responsible AI assurance

Build a review kit and teach staff to check claims, document errors, and decide whether a pilot should proceed, with synthetic nonclinical examples. [Wishlist](https://projects.hria.org/bphc/#I3-22)

## I3-03: Structured records from documents

Build an editable glossary of approved terms and synonyms to help convert reports into draft records, linked to source evidence for staff review. [Wishlist](https://projects.hria.org/bphc/#I3-03)

## I3-04: Screening clinical notes

Use a separate clinical vocabulary and test against the approved keyword baseline. Keep staff in control of review decisions and use synthetic examples unless data use is approved. [Wishlist](https://projects.hria.org/bphc/#I3-04)

## I3-07: Wastewater data feasibility

Use only authorized historical aggregates to check whether a suitable measure improves a simple influenza forecast baseline. No new sampling or live alerts in the initial work. [Wishlist](https://projects.hria.org/bphc/#I3-07)

## I3-15: Client wallet for repeat pantry intake

Prototype opt-in, verified form prefill so residents can reuse selected details. Keep standard intake available, start with one form and synthetic data, and leave cross-program sharing out. [Wishlist](https://projects.hria.org/bphc/#I3-15)

## I3-16: Editable population charts

See the [concept, agent skill, and population visualization book blueprint](proposals/i3-16/). The 3D walkthrough is a companion book project, not a grant deliverable. [Wishlist](https://projects.hria.org/bphc/#I3-16)
