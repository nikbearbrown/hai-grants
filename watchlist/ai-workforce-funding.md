# AI funding prospects for Humanitarians AI

Official sources checked November 24, 2026 (Boston). This is a prospect review, not a list of applications, partnerships, or awards. The main grants table is unchanged.

**Jobs pitch:** A counselor-assisted pilot for early-career job seekers facing employment barriers. Humanitarians AI's Jobs tool would help participants find plausible roles, identify skills gaps, and prepare for applications and interviews. Evaluate outcomes such as interviews, placements, and retention, not just application volume. Workforce partners in this document are possibilities, not commitments.

| Opportunity | Fit and latest verified access |
|---|---|
| [Cisco Global Impact Cash Grants](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/about/purpose/community-resilience/investments/global-impact-cash-grants.html) | **Screen first for Jobs.** Cisco lists an eligibility quiz and then a letter of inquiry, with a full proposal by invitation. Its current page lists up to $100,000 for first-time grantees, national or multinational operations, more than 65% economically underserved in the program's audience, and overhead generally at most 25%. Verify eligibility before drafting. Pitch direct service, not research alone or a standalone workshop. |
| [LinkedIn Future of Work Fund](https://www.socialimpact.linkedin.com/future-of-work-fund) | **Strong Jobs fit:** young adults facing employment barriers. The [2026 call](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/coop-careers-inc_check-out-our-very-own-adanta-ahanonu-and-activity-7435111342967074816-datF) closed March 15. No 2027 date found. |
| [GitLab Foundation AI for Economic Opportunity](https://www.gitlabfoundation.org/futureofwork) | **Strong Jobs fit** if the pilot serves low-income job seekers and measures economic mobility. The prior package included $250,000 and technical support. The funder says future calls will be posted on its page, but no 2027 round was found. |
| [AWS Imagine Grant Pathfinder](https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program/) | **Strong direct grant prospect.** Humanitarians AI fits the U.S. nonprofit eligibility category; educational institutions are excluded. The prior cap was $200,000 cash plus $100,000 AWS credits. Competitive if generative or agentic AI on AWS is central to the Jobs deployment, not merely added to a directory. [Round one closed June 5; invited round two closed September 14](https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program/aws-imagine-grant-faq/). AWS describes an annual cycle; no 2027 date was posted. |
| [IBM Impact Accelerator](https://www.ibm.com/responsibility/programs/ibm-impact-accelerator) | **Very strong thematic precedent for Jobs.** The [workforce RFP closed March 25, 2026](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-02-04-ibm-opens-global-rfp-for-ai-driven-solutions-shaping-the-future-of-work-and-education); IBM [announced four selections on November 10](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-11-10-ibm-impact-accelerator-brings-ai-and-quantum-to-the-future-of-learning-and-work), including Ramona Technologies' job-posting analysis and skills platform. The two-year support is primarily technology and expertise, not a cash budget for Jobs. No repeat of the workforce theme in 2027 was verified. |
| [Google.org U.S. AI Opportunity Fund](https://www.google.org/impact-report/focus-areas) | **Partner route, not a direct open call found for Jobs.** Pair career navigation with AI skills practice and a workforce delivery partner. [Project Evident's documented Accelerator](https://projectevident.org/resource/supporting-ai-adoption-among-community-based-organizations-the-ai-opportunity-accelerator-aioa/) included assistance and implementation funding in four metros, not Boston. [Goodwill Boston Career Center](https://goodwillmass.org/outlook-fall-winter-2026/) is a possible delivery partner, not a confirmed local Google-funded intake. |
| [Google.org Generative AI Accelerator](https://impactchallenge.withgoogle.com/genaiaccelerator) | **Potential direct product funding for Jobs.** The general open-call page shows selected 2025 participants. No new general 2027 round verified. The [separate 2026 government challenge](https://www.google.org/impact-challenges/ai-government-innovation/) also selected recipients. |
| [OpenAI Foundation People-First AI Fund](https://openaifoundation.org/news/2026-people-first-ai-fund) | **Conditional fit** for community workforce delivery or AI education. 2026 applications closed July 15 and [awards were announced](https://openaifoundation.org/news/2026-people-first-ai-fund-grantees). No 2027 round verified. The prior public charity operating budget range was $500,000–$10 million; confirm both Humanitarians AI's actual budget and future rules before prioritizing. |
| [Humanity AI: Communities Leading on AI](https://humanityai.ai/open-call/) | **Conditional fit** for worker-led design or governance of Jobs, or a community-led creative/education initiative. A job matcher alone is not the same as community leadership. The pooled call closed October 21, 2026; January 2027 is a status update for applicants, not a new deadline. |
| [Mozilla Democracy x AI](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-do/grantmaking/incubator/democracy-ai-cohort/) | **Better for Popper or public-interest verification than Jobs.** Mozilla lists its selected 2026 projects; no repeat call verified. A working tool with a clear civic or information-integrity use is the better fit. |
| [KPMG AI Impact Initiative](https://kpmg.com/us/en/careers-and-culture/kpmg-ai-impact-initiative.html) | **Relationship and training lead.** KPMG showcases nonprofit AI training and its work with Braven, a relevant workforce precedent; no public grant intake or 2027 round verified. |
| [Salesforce Agents for Impact](https://www.salesforce.com/nonprofit/agents-for-impact/) | **Possible platform partner.** Only prioritize if a Jobs delivery partner has a real Salesforce use case. The [2026 cohort included Year Up United](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/nonprofit-ai-pro-bono-volunteers/); no 2027 application verified. |
| [Microsoft nonprofit Azure grant](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/nonprofits/azure) | **Infrastructure credit**, not Jobs program cash. Microsoft lists $2,000 in Azure credits for eligible nonprofits. Its [separate AI for Good Washington call](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2025/01/22/microsoft-launches-5-million-ai-for-good-open-call-grant-program-to-drive-positive-change-in-washington-state/) closed in 2025 and is not a fit for a Boston-only pilot. |
| [Patrick J. McGovern Foundation](https://www.mcgovern.org/contact-the-patrick-j-mcgovern-foundation/) | **Invitation only.** No unsolicited requests. Keep as long-term relationship context, not an application. |

A [separate Project Evident opportunity](https://projectevident.org/resource/career-exploration-evidence-fund-invitation-for-letters-of-interest/) has a December 15, 2026 letter-of-interest deadline but requires a formal school connection and service to students in grades 6–10. That is not the current early-career Jobs pilot. Consider only with genuine delivery and evidence for that population.
