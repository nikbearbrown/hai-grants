# Funder checks for the November 24, 2026 watchlist

All 13 leads from the November 24 alert were already listed in the [generated watchlist](README.md). Use the checks below before acting on that snapshot; a date marked rolling or unstated in the automated feed does not establish that applications are open. None of these checks is a decision to apply.

| Lead | Funder check and relevance to Humanitarians AI |
| --- | --- |
| [IEEE / UNDP GenAI for Good Challenge: Cities Edition](https://ieeeht.org/get-involved/funding-opportunities/genai-for-good/) | **Dec 13, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET.** Up to $40,000 per winning team across prototype and pilot stages. Eligible applicants are informal teams of at least two adults, not organizations. A team could explore a source-traceable transit evidence assistant for Pristina; participants would need to choose a designated city brief, use GENIE.AI, and finish both submission components. |
| [Varkey Foundation Global Schools Prize](https://www.varkeyfoundation.org/global-schools-prize) | **Dec 16, 2026.** Schools teaching students up to age 18 apply; Humanitarians AI could help an eligible school document proven impact, not apply as a standalone nonprofit. AI Transformation and STEM Education are categories. $1 million is the total prize pool, not a grant to each school. |
| [EDA FY 2027 Build to Scale](https://www.eda.gov/funding/funding-opportunities/fiscal-year-2027-build-scale) | **Feb 26, 2027, 4:59 p.m. ET.** Up to $1M (Emerging) or $7M (Growth). Nonprofit applicants need state or local government support and a [1:1 match](https://www.eda.gov/sites/default/files/2026-11/FY27_B2S_NOFO_Final.pdf). Consider only with regional partners and a credible technology commercialization plan, not a stand-alone jobs board or workshop. |
| [Anthropic Claude Corps](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/host) | Already in the [main tracker](../anthropic/). Host applications are advertised as rolling; the specific cohort currently accepting hosts was not confirmed. Fellow placements and the $10,000 implementation grant are distinct from a general cash grant. |
| [Microsoft for Nonprofits: Azure](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/nonprofits/azure) | **$2,000 per year in Azure credits** for eligible nonprofits, not cash. Potential hosting cost offset; [usage beyond the credit is billed](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/nonprofits/faq). |
| [Google for Nonprofits](https://www.google.com/nonprofits/) | Google confirms nonprofit Workspace and AI benefits, including Gemini and Gemini Notebook through its [Workspace program](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/17049066?hl=en). The claimed general $2,000 annual Google Cloud credit was not verified; do not budget around it. |
| [AWS Imagine Grant: U.S. Pathfinder](https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program/) | **2026–27 intake closed.** First round closed June 5 and the invited round closed September 14. Pathfinder lists up to $200,000 in cash plus $100,000 in AWS credits. Watch for the next cycle rather than treating this as rolling. |
| [Omidyar Network Tech Journalism Fund](https://omidyar.com/where-we-focus/tech-journalism-fund/) | **Closed to new applications.** The fund wound down effective Nov 1, 2026. Disregard the Dec 31 date in the alert. The funder points to a separate Reporters in Residence program expected to accept applications in early 2027. |
| [UNICEF Venture Fund](https://www.unicefventurefund.org/apply-funding) | Rolling call for eligible **for-profit** technology companies registered in UNICEF programme countries, with an open-source prototype and early pilot results. Up to $100,000 equity-free; not a direct fit for Humanitarians AI. |
| GSMA Innovation Fund for AI-Enabled Financial Health | Third-party reports identify eligible for-profit enterprises serving low- and middle-income markets. The [GSMA terms](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GSMA-Innovation-Fund-for-AI-Enabled-Financial-Health-TCs.pdf) could not be retrieved for this check. Treat the reported date and award as provisional; not a direct nonprofit lead. |
| [Microsoft AI for Humanitarian Action](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/msc/documents/CSR/Digital-Pathways.pdf) | Microsoft documents the program, but a current open application was not verified. Do not conflate it with the Azure nonprofit credit. |
| [Mozilla Technology Fund](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-fund/programs/mozilla-technology-fund-mtf/) | Prior awards are documented; no current intake was verified. Separate from the [Creative Advocacy fellowship](../mozilla-foundation/) already tracked. |
| US Funders: Tech, Media & Digital Rights | A directory for finding leads, not a distinct award or application. Do not count it as an open opportunity. |
