# XPRIZE

## Accelerate Learning

**Status:** Logged for screening; no registration or submission. This is a competition with a **$6 million total prize purse**, not a $6 million grant to a single applicant. XPRIZE lists nonprofits as eligible entrants. Teams co-design and test with schools or districts in India or Sub-Saharan Africa.

| Track | Prize purse | Target | Registration and qualifying submission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Last Mile Moonshot | $1 million | Offline-capable technology co-designed with teachers; at least a 50 percentage-point mastery gain for under $40 per first grader per year | February 15, 2027 |
| Design for Scale Core | $5 million | District-scale delivery; at least a 25 percentage-point mastery gain for under $10 per first grader per year | March 15, 2027 |

Dates are preliminary. December 11, 2026 is the guidelines feedback cutoff shown by XPRIZE, not the competition entry deadline.

**Potential fit:** Lyrical Literacy is a possible Moonshot starting point for foundational reading, not a complete submission. A credible team would also need a numeracy approach, local-language adaptation, offline delivery, teacher co-design, a measurement plan for both skills, and a cost model that holds under field conditions. Check the current guidelines, fees, regional eligibility rules and financing needs before committing. No partner or delivery capacity is assumed.
