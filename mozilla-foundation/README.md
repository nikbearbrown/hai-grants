# Mozilla Foundation

## 2027 Independent Fellowship: Creative Advocacy

**Status:** Screening; no letter submitted. One Independent Fellowship slot is reserved for Creative Advocacy. Awards are paid to individuals, not organizations: **$100,000 stipend plus $25,000 project budget**. Existing employment is allowed if the fellow can deliver the project and participate in the program. The fellowship begins June 1, 2027 and runs for 12 months.

**Letter of intent:** Due **December 17, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific (December 18 at 2:59 a.m. Eastern)**. Full applications are by invitation only. Mozilla lists February 16 for invitations and March 12 for full applications. The FAQ exempts Creative Advocacy from the general expectation that projects be primarily technical.

**Possible project:** Co-design a public creative campaign with independent musicians that exposes how paid playlist offers are pitched and who benefits. Start with a workshop in which artists compare offers and build a practical checklist. Add a participatory public artifact and reusable open materials so the work reaches people beyond the first session. Do not imply collaborators are committed or that paid offers are all equivalent.
