**Potential fit:** C-STEP is the closer fit if a school-system partner has an evidence-to-practice problem that Humanitarians AI can help solve. Computational Skepticism could check the evidence behind recommendations, and Frictional could measure the work of reviewing and adopting them. For 84.324T, prioritize only with teacher-preparation and special-education research expertise. Its application needs co-design and cost-sharing plans; both must be in place by the end of Year 1 for continuation, including cash or in-kind cost sharing equal to at least 5% of federal funds.

**Before pursuing:** Confirm the applicant and its SAM.gov and Grants.gov registration status, a research lead, a suitable preK–12 or teacher-preparation setting, and capacity to prepare the federal application. No partner or staffing commitments are assumed.
