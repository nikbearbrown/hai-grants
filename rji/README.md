# Reynolds Journalism Institute

## 2027 Professional Innovation Fellowship: Individual

**Status:** Logged for screening; nothing submitted. First round closes **January 8, 2027**. RJI's public listing does not specify a cutoff time. A January 6 Q&A is listed for 11 a.m. Central / noon Eastern. The eight-month fellowship is mostly remote, starts June 28, 2027 with an in-person kickoff in Columbia, Missouri, and covers required program travel. The **$75,000 stipend** goes to the individual in three installments tied to deliverables and helps cover project costs. Researchers and others with full-time jobs can apply for work outside their regular job.

**Deliverable:** Build, test with newsroom feedback, and publicly launch a free, accessible, open-source resource that journalists across the U.S. can use. RJI also expects public updates, launch planning, and a sustainability plan.

**Possible project:** Adapt Computational Skepticism into a newsroom tool that ties claims in draft stories to public records, flags unsupported statements, and hands the evidence to an editor for review. Demonstrate it with one task, such as checking denominators, dates, units, and suppressed values in a local budget story against its source spreadsheet, then test whether other newsrooms can use it without custom setup. Before committing, confirm a newsroom testing partner, maintenance ownership, project boundaries with regular employment, and whether the first payment covers planned launch costs.

**Related tracks:** RJI separately lists a $75,000 Institutional Fellowship for an embedded project and a $100,000 Emerging Technology Fellowship for a project that genuinely depends on emerging technology. Deadline is the same; neither is assumed to be the intended track.
