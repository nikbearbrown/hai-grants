# Cabot Family Charitable Trust

**Status:** Screening; no application submitted. The next published-cycle deadline is **February 1, 2027**, with decisions in June. Typical one-year awards are **$5,000–$50,000**. The Trust funds arts, education, and youth development, among other areas.

**Eligibility:** The applicant must be a 501(c)(3) based in the Trust's geographic area, serve Boston or an eligible surrounding community, and not have applied in the preceding 12 months. Current grantees must submit their report by the application deadline. Confirm Humanitarians AI's location and submission history before proceeding; event sponsorships are excluded.

**Potential fit:** A local Lyrical Literacy pilot with a school, library, or community partner. Lead with direct services for children and families: educator-reviewed, song-based early literacy activities delivered over multiple sessions, with participation and learning tracked separately. Budget for delivery, accessibility, and assessment. No site or partner is confirmed.

**Precedent:** The 2025 annual report, p. 5 of the PDF, lists a $30,000 award to Arts for Learning Massachusetts for Expanding Horizons Through Music, an early-literacy program for children experiencing housing insecurity and poverty. This indicates program fit, not an expected award.
