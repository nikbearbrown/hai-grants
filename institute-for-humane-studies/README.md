# Institute for Humane Studies

## AI and the Workforce

**Status:** Screening; no application submitted. The call invites academic scholars to study how AI changes employment, productivity, occupations, economic opportunity, or innovation. **Deadline: December 15, 2026**; no cutoff time is given on the public call. Decisions are scheduled for January 15, 2027. Awards are typically up to **$20,000**; IHS may consider larger exceptional requests. Screen as a faculty-led project rather than assume Humanitarians AI is the grantee.

**Costs:** Research assistance, dataset purchases, specialized research LLM subscriptions, and eligible dissemination costs are allowed. Hardware, indirect costs or institutional overhead, living expenses, and retroactive expenses are excluded. Before committing, confirm the award recipient and institutional handling, and keep any journal selection consistent with Northeastern's publishing coverage.

**Possible project:** Extend [The Junior's Curve](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/the-juniors-curve) work on how agents affect early-career developers. Randomly assign comparable coding tasks to agent-assisted or unaided conditions and score immediate correctness and human active minutes in implementation and verification. Then use a delayed, unaided debugging task on new code to measure transfer. Report performance, time allocation, and retention separately. Treat the shift toward verification as a hypothesis; job postings do not measure work time. Budget for research assistance, blinded scoring, and eligible research tools. Obtain applicable research ethics review before recruitment or data collection.
