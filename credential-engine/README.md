# Credential Engine: Connected Course Data evaluator

**Status:** Opportunity logged; not submitted. The [RFP](https://credentialengine.org/2026/11/24/request-for-proposals-for-the-connected-course-data-evaluator/) requests a response of up to two pages by email to Jeff Grann by December 11, 2026. It gives no cutoff time. The evaluation budget is up to $30,000, with evaluator travel to project events covered separately.

Credential Engine is testing a workflow that extracts learning objectives and assessments from course materials using AI and represents them in the Credential Transparency Description Language (CTDL). It seeks an independent evaluator to define quality measures up front and share findings during the project. The pilot spans at least three institutions; source materials come from LMS Common Cartridge exports and may also include competencies and rubrics. Institutions review and approve their records before publication to the Credential Registry.

## Potential Humanitarians AI approach

- **Frictional: measure the work of getting to publication.** Record active review minutes, elapsed approval time, corrections, handoffs, and rework per course. Separate review effort by role; distinguish time spent working from time waiting. If feasible, compare with a comparable manual workflow.
- **Computational Skepticism: verify the output.** Compare extracted objectives and assessments to reviewer-annotated source materials, track unsupported additions and omissions, and check traceability and fidelity after CTDL conversion. Check whether the records support realistic tasks for learners, advisors, faculty, and employers.
- **Independent evaluation:** Agree on measures and reviewer rules before scoring, disclose relevant relationships, and keep the evaluation separate from development of the workflow under review.

Use permissioned course materials and minimize retained data. No college participation, evaluator staffing, or submission is assumed.

## Scope and decision points

- Contract by December 31, 2026; evaluation plan and instruments in January 2027.
- Four in-person Build-a-thons from March through June, each focused on a different audience. Institutional review and publication continue through summer. Interim findings are shared during the project; final report in Q4 2027.
- The response needs named roles, examples of prior evaluation work, earliest availability, budget fit and constraints, and disclosure of any implementation or facilitation role that could affect independence.
- Before deciding whether to apply, confirm who can cover the workshops and reporting through Q4 within budget, and disclose any actual project overlap. Related Humanitarians AI course-tooling experience is relevant but does not by itself establish involvement in this project.
