# Work with Humanitarians AI

Pick an opportunity from the [table](README.md) and tell us what you would like to do. We welcome community partners, educators, researchers, designers, engineers, and people with relevant lived or operational experience.

For research manuscripts, use [Humanitarians AI Research](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/hai-research).

## Express interest privately

Reply to the person who invited you. Put the active opportunity name in the subject. In a few lines, share:

- Which idea you want to work on and why.
- The concrete role or contribution you would like to make, with an optional link to public work.
- Your availability and any time or location constraints.
- Whether you are a current Humanitarians AI fellow interested in being considered for a nomination, a prospective advisor, or proposing a community or institutional partnership.

An expression of interest is not a nomination, application, offer, or promise of funding. The sponsor's rules, team fit and availability determine whether a role moves forward. Do not send work authorization documents, sensitive data, or participant information in this first message.

## Give public feedback

[Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/hai-grants/issues/new) for a general project question, a correction, or a suggestion you are comfortable sharing publicly. Use synthetic examples. Keep personal information, private staffing discussions, and sponsor or participant data off GitHub. Do not contact a sponsor on behalf of Humanitarians AI without authorization.
